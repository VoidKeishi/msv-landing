# Service Delivery Model - 1 page
MSV offers a flexible service delivery model aligned to project stage, risk profile, and client objectives. We engage as an advisor, EPCM contractor, or embedded team, providing practical, value-driven solutions across the mining project lifecycle.

Engagement Options

Advisory
Independent technical and commercial advice supporting decision-making across exploration, development, and operations. Typical scopes include technical reviews, study support, owner’s team advisory, and cost and schedule validation.

EPCM
Delivery of Engineering, Procurement and Construction Management services, providing integrated control of engineering, procurement, construction oversight, and commissioning while maintaining owner visibility and governance.

Embedded Team
MSV personnel embedded within client teams to provide targeted technical, project delivery, or operational capability, ensuring continuity, knowledge transfer, and alignment with site objectives.

Phase-Based Support

MSV supports projects across all stages:
    • Exploration – program design, execution, and reporting
    • Studies & Development – resource and reserve development, mine planning, and project definition
    • Execution – EPCM delivery and owner’s team representation
    • Operations – operational readiness, ramp-up, and optimisation

Engagements may cover a single phase or span multiple stages, maintaining continuity and accountability throughout the project lifecycle.

Commercial Structures

Fee-for-Service
MSV’s primary model is a transparent fee-for-service structure with clearly defined scopes, deliverables, and performance outcomes.

Equity Participation
For select projects, MSV may consider equity or equity-linked participation, typically alongside reduced fees, where MSV is providing material technical or delivery value and long-term alignment is appropriate.

Fit-for-Purpose Delivery
Across all engagement models, MSV delivers practical, cost-effective solutions to international standards, aligned with local operating conditions and project objectives.
