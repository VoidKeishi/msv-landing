# Core Competencies - 2 pages
MSV delivers integrated mining services across the full project lifecycle, from early-stage exploration through project development and into operations. Our capability is built on hands-on regional experience, enabling the delivery of technically robust, cost-effective, and operationally fit-for-purpose solutions aligned with project objectives and local operating conditions.

MSV’s core competencies include:

    • Exploration & Geological Services
    • Resource & Reserve Development
    • Project Delivery (Owner’s Team & EPCM)
    • Operational Readiness & Support

Exploration & Geological Services

MSV provides an end-to-end exploration capability, combining program design, execution, and technical reporting using in-house teams, equipment, and specialist support. This integrated approach improves data quality, schedule control, and cost efficiency.

Key services include:
    • Grassroots exploration, including geological mapping, soil and rock-chip sampling, trenching, and technical reporting
    • Geophysical surveys, including EM, IP, and DHEM program design, implementation, specialist interpretation, and reporting
    • Drilling services, from target generation and drill program design through to execution, with the MSV team having delivered in excess of 150,000 metres of diamond drilling
    • In-house core logging, sampling, laboratory management, and data preparation to support geological modelling and reporting
    • Ownership and operation of diamond drilling and geophysical equipment, enabling efficient and cost-effective deployment
    • Geological database management and technical reporting to meet client, regulatory, and study requirements

Resource & Reserve Development

MSV has strong capability in geological modelling, resource estimation, and reserve development, supported by industry-standard software including Surpac and Micromine.

Our team develops geological and resource models in accordance with local reporting requirements, as well as JORC and NI43-101 standards. Where required, MSV’s Senior Technical Advisor is able to act as a Competent Person, providing sign-off for resource statements under both codes.

For reserve development, MSV undertakes mine optimisation and reserve conversion studies using an extensive internal database of mining and processing costs. Reserve statements can be certified to JORC and NI43-101 standards, with sign-off provided by MSV’s Senior Advisor, who is recognised as a JORC and NI43-101 Competent Person.

Project Delivery

MSV has the flexibility to operate on either side of the project delivery interface, acting as an Owner’s Representative or as an EPCM contractor, depending on client needs and project stage.

As Owner’s Representative, MSV oversees engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning, ensuring delivery against scope, schedule, cost, quality, safety, and performance objectives.

As an EPCM contractor, MSV manages engineering disciplines, procurement activities, contractors, and suppliers, delivering projects efficiently and to the required technical and safety standards.

With a deep understanding of the mining value chain, MSV is well positioned to manage projects from early exploration through development and into operations, delivering fit-for-purpose solutions aligned with local conditions and commercial objectives.

Operational Readiness & Support

MSV’s team has direct operational experience across mining projects in Southeast Asia and globally, providing practical insight into the transition from project development to steady-state operations.

MSV supports clients in establishing robust and compliant operating organisations, including:

    • Human resources systems, recruitment, and workforce administration
    • Procurement, logistics, and warehousing functions
    • Finance, accounting, and reporting systems
    • Corporate governance, policies, and procedures
    • Safety, environmental, and compliance management systems

From early operational planning through to steady-state operations, MSV supports clients in building efficient, sustainable, and well-governed mining operations.
